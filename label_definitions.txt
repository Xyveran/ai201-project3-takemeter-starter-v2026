Fluff: Offers an opinion, reaction, or comment about the dicussion topic without providing supporting reasoning, evidence, context, or a distinct argument, and without changing the subject.
Substantive: Contributes relevant information, personal experience, specific examples, reasoning, or a question that develops the discussion topic or responds to another participant.
Derailing: Shifts attention away from the discussion's central topic or argument by introducing an unrelated subject, targeting a person or group with a dismissive remark, or making a personal attack instead of addressing the topic of discussion.
